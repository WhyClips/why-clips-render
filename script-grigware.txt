# 5 deliberate pauses (0.5s) at segment breaks — marked [PAUSE].

[SEG1 — hook, mid-action, NO title card]
One morning in 1910, six prisoners broke out of Leavenworth prison — with a gun made of wood.
[PAUSE]

[SEG2 — setting + escalation]
Frank Grigware was twenty-three, a baseball player who'd robbed a mail train with four friends — seven sacks of mail, seven hundred dollars — and got life for it.
Leavenworth wasn't even finished yet. The prisoners were building their own prison, stone by stone, like ants building their own anthill.
Then a supply train backed into the yard — and the six men struck. They pulled the wooden gun, grabbed the engineer, and drove that train straight through the prison gates.
Five were caught within days. Frank was the only one who got away.
[PAUSE]

[SEG3 — mid-story question]
So how do you hide for twenty-four years?
[PAUSE]

[SEG4 — "You see" pivot]
You see, he just became someone else. He crossed into Canada, opened a candy store, built houses, got married — and in 1916, the town elected him mayor.
[PAUSE]

[SEG5 — escalation + twist 1]
For twenty-four years, the most wanted man in America was hiding in plain sight, shaking hands at church on Sunday.
Then one tiny mistake undid it all. Police fingerprinted him for trapping foxes out of season — and the prints matched a twenty-four-year-old FBI file.
[PAUSE]

[SEG6 — double-twist payoff, last line = twist, END on it]
The mayor was the escaped lifer. But the town didn't turn him in. They raised ten thousand dollars for his bail, flooded Ottawa and Washington with protests — and America quietly dropped the case. He never spent another day in prison, and died a free man at ninety-one.
