## [HOOK — 0-3s]
One email. Four hundred people. Thirteen million dollars. This is the story of the very first spam.

## [SETUP]
His name was Gary Thuerk. In nineteen seventy-eight, he was a marketing manager at DEC — a computer company. No Gmail. No smartphones. Just giant humming computers in cold rooms. And on the third of May, he did something nobody had ever done before. He sent one email to about four hundred people on ARPANET — the military network that became the internet — advertising DEC's brand-new computers.

## [THE MOCKER — the backlash]
People were furious. ARPANET was for scientists and soldiers — not salesmen. Complaints poured in from every corner of the network. His boss was so embarrassed, he made Gary promise never to do it again.

## [ESCALATION 1 — it worked]
But here's the thing nobody expected. About forty people showed up to the demos. Forty people — from a single email.

## [ESCALATION 2 — the money]
And those demos turned into sales. Gary says that one email sold twelve to thirteen million dollars' worth of computers. In nineteen seventy-eight!

## ["YOU SEE,"]
You see, Gary didn't just invent spam. He invented email marketing — and he's still proud of it. Guinness World Records lists his email as the oldest spam on Earth.

## [PAYOFF]
So the next time your inbox fills up with junk, remember Gary Thuerk. One email. Four hundred people. Thirteen million dollars.

## [LAST LINE — biggest reveal, no moral after]
The first spam email in history... was also the most successful email ever sent.
