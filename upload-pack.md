## Facts (all from the candidates file, verified in research)
- 1573, Panama: Drake stood on a hill and saw the Atlantic and the Pacific at once, led by cimarrones (runaway enslaved Africans).
- Spain's silver crossed the isthmus on mule trains; enslaved Africans escaped and built free villages — the cimarrones, "the wild ones".
- Spain sent armies to recapture them; the cimarrones vanished into the jungle and kept raiding the treasure road.
- 1572: Francis Drake arrived to steal Spain's silver; the cimarrones found HIM. Guide Pedro Mandinga offered the deal: we guide you, you share the silver.
- February 1573: 30 cimarrones guided Drake across the isthmus, ambushed a mule train, hid the silver in a riverbed.
- Twist: the first Englishman to see the Pacific from Panama was shown it by the slaves Spain couldn't catch.
