## Title
NASA Flew 500 Seeds Around the Moon — The Trees Are Still Standing

## Description
In 1971, NASA flew 500 tree seeds around the Moon — and the trees they grew into are still standing in America today. 🌙🌲

The seeds never even touched the Moon. They circled it 34 times, then burst open on the way home — and everyone thought the experiment was dead. What happened next turned them into the longest-living souvenirs of the Moon landings.

Would you plant a tree that went to space? 👇
